Key recommendations

  • Protect Google Search's strong performance while checking capacity and lead quality.
  • Run controlled, incremental budget tests before materially scaling or cutting any channel.
  • Investigate lower-return channels for targeting, conversion quality, and attribution issues.
  • Validate assumptions against actual CRM outcomes and reconciled finance data.
